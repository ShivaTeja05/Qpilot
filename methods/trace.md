# Method card: Trace (from a result back to the wells to look at)

**What it answers.** A scientist looks at one result (a gene's call), one sample or one well and asks which input made it this way, and whether it matters. Trace takes the arithmetic as right and walks back through the numbers the result was built from: conclusion → group set → sample value → target well / normaliser → reference well → plate controls. It ends at a physical place (plate, well, tube) with what is unusual there, what each option would do to the result, and whether that changes the call. It gives one of four answers: *a place to look*, *a choice to look at* (the wells show nothing that changes the result, but changing one analysis choice alone flips it), *nothing to look at* (the influential samples are listed with "nothing unusual"), or *absent* (the sample is in no comparison).

**References.** Robust z (median and 1.4826 × MAD). The normalisation factor as a quality signal: Vermeulen et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 39: e63 (2011). LinRegPCR's well notes: Ruijter et al., *Nucleic Acids Research* 37: e45 (2009). The 5-cycle NTC window: MIQE 2.0. Kinetic outlier detection (the idea behind S4): Bar et al. 2003. The walk and the checks S1, S6 and S7 are qPilot's own.

**Implementation.** `qpilot/qpcr/trace.py` (`build_context`, `trace_gene`, `trace_sample`, `trace_well`, `trace_store`, `exclusion_effect`, `TraceResult.card`, `TRACE_THRESHOLDS`, `METHOD_CARD`); the consequence classes in `qpilot/core/consequence.py`; fixed-group what-if evaluation in `qpilot/qpcr/space.py` (`QpcrSpace.cell_arrays`, `group_masks`, `evaluate_arrays`, `evaluate_with`); the panel in `qpilot/explain/trace_text.py`; the planted-fault benchmark in `qpilot/qpcr/trace_bench.py`.

| Step | Definition | Cut-off |
|---|---|---|
| Groups | Held fixed in every scenario: the primary cut's masks. Leave-one-out, reading a reference from the others, setting a sample aside and bringing one back never re-derive the groups | — |
| Influence of a sample value | Leave-one-out Welch in closed form from centred group sums; results this close to the call rule are recomputed exactly | `boundary_tol` 1e-9 |
| Influence of a reference well | Its value replaced by the expected value moves every relative value of the sample by the deviation ÷ k; closed form, all samples × references × genes | — |
| Walk | Depth-first in order of influence; a child is pruned when it moves the result by less than a share of the result's CI half-width **and** no check fails below it **and** it does not change the call. Failed checks are known per subtree before the walk | `eps_ci_share` 0.1 |
| S1 reference well | Expected value of reference r: mean of the sample's other references + r's offset (median over the compared samples of r minus the mean of the others). The sample's single reference with the largest robust \|z\| is flagged. Needs three or more references | \|z\| > 3.5 |
| S2 whole sample late | Per sample, median over its wells of Cq minus the plate's median Cq (a plate is one target on one run); robust z, late side | z > 3.5 |
| S4 whole-sample efficiency | The same with the well's efficiency; shown only when both rules hold | \|z\| > 3.5 and \|shift\| > 0.05 |
| S5 curve note | A LinRegPCR note on a well that carries a value. `Ncopy < 10` and `Ncopy > 100 Mio` describe the amount, not the curve, and never fail; free-text notes are mapped to fixed codes | any other note |
| S6 NTC window | A study well with Cq ≥ (lowest NTC Cq of its plate − window). "Nothing found" is claimed per plate, only where no NTC amplified | 5 cycles |
| S7 absent | A sample in no comparison: the empty reference or marker wells (addresses and notes). Restore (one empty reference only): fill it with the mean of the other references + its offset; the group by the fixed marker threshold | — |
| Options | `keep`; `read_from_other_refs` (reference wells); `set_aside_value` (a target well: only that value); `set_aside` (the sample, from every comparison). Each is evaluated exactly with the groups fixed | — |
| Consequence | `changes_call` > `beyond_ci` (the fold change moves by more than its CI half-width) > `changes_nothing`; for the root gene, and the changed calls of every gene | — |
| Ranking | Class, then the size of the change, then \|z\|, then sample id | — |
| Facts | Every number shown: `f_trace_<root>_<rank>_<field>` and `f_trace_<root>_<field>`; sample ids, plates and wells are string facts | — |

**What we changed from the plan.** S3 (references shifted, targets not) is cut, as the plan says. S8 needs a sample sheet with annotations and is listed as what the data cannot show; S9 is not applicable without technical replicates. `read_from_other_refs` is offered for every reference-well place, not only S1 ones. A place is an address: a sample flagged both late (S2) and near an NTC (S6) gives two places, its tube and its well. In a well trace, S1 fails on the well when its own \|z\| exceeds the threshold. The "other reference wells agree with each other" line is judged without the flagged well (S1 run on the remaining references), so one far-off well does not drag the others' expected values.

**Planted-fault benchmark (plan T3).** `qpilot/qpcr/trace_bench.py` (`plant`, `run_benchmark`, `bench_items`, `run_case`, `prepare_baseline`, `TraceScorecard`). Each case copies the per-well table, plants one fault at a seeded address, rebuilds the study model from the planted table (groups, reference offsets and robust thresholds re-derived, as for a real run) and runs Trace from the fault's entry. The planted Cq and N0 stay consistent: LinRegPCR reports N0 = Nq / E^Cq with E the gene's mean efficiency on the plate (on the demo n0 · E^Cq is constant within each plate to a few parts in 10⁴ of a log10 unit), so a shift of d cycles gives n0 · E^−d. F3 also lowers the well's own efficiency (`eff_indiv`, which S4 reads) by 0.10; the plate's mean is held, because one well moves it by at most 0.10 over the plate's ~350 wells, LinRegPCR leaves a well more than 0.05 from the plate median out of that mean, and a change of it would scale every well of the plate nearly alike. LinRegPCR is not re-run, so the notes it would add (for F3, "PCR efficiency outlier") are not added and S5 is not exercised. Addresses whose planted Cq would pass the latest Cq the table reports (47.4 on the demo) are not drawn. Samples are drawn from the compared samples that S1, S2 and S4 do not already flag (18 such real faults on the demo, among them 2016, 1924 and 1772); F4 plates from the reference and compared genes' plates whose NTCs are clean (52 of 64: not the marker's plate, which feeds no compared value, and not the 11 plates with an amplifying NTC, ALUsq's among them). The same trace on the unplanted table gives the known places: a place at the same address with no new check is set aside from the ranking and from the false-place count. The hit is the planted reference well (F1), the sample's tube, a whole-sample place (F2, F3), a study well of the planted plate within the NTC window (F4), the planted well among the wells that left the sample out (F5). F6 is scored as a refusal: the well trace says Trace cannot tell from biology without replicates (`summary["limit"]`, new in the well trace, with a fact and the panel line), claims no check on the well that it did not show before, and the gene's trace adds no place on that sample.

Where it falls short, and what the numbers do not say:
- **F2 falls short of the plan (20 % against 90 %).** S2 is a study-relative robust z: on this panel the scale is 1.24 cycles (46 tumours are more than two cycles late on their median gene), so S2 needs a median shift of more than 4.3 cycles. A +3-cycle plant reaches that only in tumours already 1.5–2.8 cycles late (the 10 hits); the 40 misses started at most 1.3 cycles late. S1 never fires on it (the references move together). On this panel +3 cycles of low input sits inside the normal spread; a fixed cycle rule would flag far more tumours (§8 of the plan). The 6 false places in F2 (in 5 cases) are wells of the planted tumour that the shift pushed into an NTC window (S6); the one in F3 is the same.
- **The sample entry starts at the planted sample.** F1, F2, F3 and F5 measure whether Trace names the right well or tube once a scientist asks about that tumour. Study-wide, with the real faults set aside, the planted sample is the first newly flagged one in 49/50 (F1), 10/50 (F2) and 50/50 (F3) cases. With the real faults competing it is first in 3/50 (F1; mostly fourth, behind 2016, 1924 and 2753, as in the T1 synthetic row), 0/50 (F2) and 50/50 (F3, since no real fault shifts efficiency).
- **The one F1 miss** is tumour 2711's UBC(2) well, which read 3.4 log2 above its expectation before planting (z +2.2); +8 cycles left it at z −2.5. UBC(2) is the noisiest reference (robust scale 1.51 log2).
- **F3 is found through S4 every time** (an efficiency shift of −0.10 against a study range of −0.02…+0.03; \|z\| 15.6–20.4), never through S2 (+2 cycles). On a real run LinRegPCR's own efficiency notes would also show on those wells.
- **F4 names the plate, not one well.** The NTC at Cq 30 puts the plate's later study wells within the 5-cycle window (median 335 wells per case, 93–347), all on the planted plate, so no false places. A plate whose study wells all come up before Cq 25 would show no place (none of the 52 is: each has at least 73 compared wells at Cq 25 or later). 4 of the 50 cases were reference plates, traced from a gene the seed draws.
- **Most planted faults change no call, and Trace says so.** The place at the planted address changes a call in 14 of the 49 F1 wells found, 1 of the 10 F2 tubes found, and in none of the F3 tubes or F4 plates: normalisation by the sample's own references absorbs most of a whole-sample shift, and an NTC moves no value. Trace still names the place and ranks it by what it does to the result.
- **F6:** in every case the well trace says it cannot tell from biology without replicates and nothing new is claimed; in 5 of 50 the planted sample appears in its gene's "influential, nothing unusual" list, which is the honest answer.
- **New flags elsewhere** are samples sitting at a threshold that tip when the planted table re-derives the robust statistics: only ever 2709 (S2 z 3.49 before planting; in 44 of the 300 cases) and 2758 (S1 \|z\| 3.47 on HMBS; in 21).
- Timing, one process: 0.26 s per case (model rebuild 0.02 s, trace context 0.16 s, the traces); F4 0.51 s, because each of its hundreds of places has its options evaluated exactly; 300 cases in 77 s.
- Not done here: the harness family (items, scoring in `harness/report.py`, the record) is wired by the integration step from `bench_items` and `run_case`.

**What is not claimed.** Trace never names a cause: each line says what the data look like, which bench step that is consistent with and what to check. A single target well that is off cannot be told apart from biology without replicates. A fault shared by every sample of one group looks like biology. It cannot see the wrong sample in a tube, RNA integrity, RT replicates or no-RT controls. In the demo layout each sample sits at the same well on every plate, so a sample and its well position are confounded; Trace lists the plates where the same position is late (K22: HMBS and NHLH2) as context and never blames the instrument block. Thresholds are study-relative; a robust z with a MAD of zero gives no flag. With technical replicates a place names the first of its wells. Trace only reads: every change is a person's decision. In a Studio session a person's Decide… on a place applies a set-aside as wells left out of the analysis (curve exclusions accepted with the reason; the study is analysed again); for a study analysed outside Studio it is recorded on a `trace_set_aside` card and the numbers stay as analysed. Reading a well from the sample's other reference wells is a what-if only.
